[Lawyer]  (tokens: 123 in, 361 out)
**Rights** – You may choose whether or not to give money; no one can force a payment unless a valid contract or court order exists.  

**Risks** – Paying without documentation can leave you vulnerable to claims of a loan, gift, or fraud. If the other party later disputes the amount or purpose, you may have no proof of your intentions.  

**What to Put in Writing** –  
1. **Written agreement** stating the amount, purpose (gift, loan, or payment for services), repayment terms (if any), and any conditions.  
2. **Signatures** of both parties and date.  
3. **Proof of delivery** (receipt, bank record).  
4. **Clarify** that you are not admitting liability for any unrelated claims.  

Keep a copy for your records; it protects both parties and provides evidence if a dispute arises.
